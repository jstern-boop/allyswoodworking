Try it (demo instructions)

  1. Click Open the Sheet at the top. It opens the demo Google Sheet in your Google account.
  2. Mark something sold: change a Status cell to Sold. A "SOLD" ribbon appears.
  3. Hide something: change Status to Hidden. It disappears from the shop.
  4. Add a product: fill in a new row. Leave Photo blank for a wood-grain placeholder, or paste a photo link.
  5. Feature it: type Yes in Featured to put it in the top row.
  6. Come back here. The page refreshes itself every 15 seconds, or press F5.

Photo links: upload a photo to Google Drive, set it to "Anyone with the link can view," copy the link, and paste it into the Photo cell. Any public image link (ending in .jpg or .png) also works.
